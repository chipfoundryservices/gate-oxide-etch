## A.2 CF₄ Dissociation Products

**Primary Dissociation**: CF₄ → CF₃• + F•

**Secondary Products**:
- CF₃•, CF₂•, CF•
- F•, F₂
- CF₃⁺, CF₂⁺ (ions)

**Typical Gas-Phase Composition** (CCP 100W, 100 mTorr):
- Unreacted CF₄: 60-80%
- F•: 15-25%
- CF₃•: 5-10%
- CF₂•: 2-5%
- Ions (CF₃⁺, CF₂⁺, F⁺): <1%
