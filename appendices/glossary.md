# Glossary: Gate Oxide Etch-Specific Terminology

## A

**Anisotropy**: Measure of directional preference in etch (1.0 = perfectly vertical, 0.0 = isotropic)
**Aspect Ratio**: Feature height divided by width (e.g., 5:1 for trench)
**ARDE**: Aspect Ratio Dependent Etching - feature size-dependent etch rate variation

## B

**Bias Power**: Separate RF power applied to wafer/substrate for ion energy control
**Breakdown Field**: Electric field strength at which dielectric fails (SiO₂: >10 MV/cm)
**BTI**: Bias Temperature Instability - reliability degradation under gate bias stress

## C

**CCP**: Capacitively Coupled Plasma - standard 13.56 MHz plasma source
**C₄F₈**: Octafluorocyclobutane - polymer-forming gas for selectivity enhancement
**Capacitive Coupling**: Direct capacitive connection between electrode and plasma
**Channel**: Conductive path in transistor controlled by gate voltage
**CHF₃**: Fluoroform - reactive fluorine source alternative to CF₄
**Dit**: Interface State Density - trap state density at oxide-silicon interface (units: cm⁻² eV⁻¹)

## D

**Debye Sheath**: Transition region between plasma and electrode/wall
**Direct Tunneling**: Quantum tunneling of electrons through thin oxide (primary leakage at advanced nodes)
**Dissociation**: Breaking of molecular bonds in plasma (CF₄ → CF₃ + F•)

## E

**Electron Temperature (Te)**: Measure of electron kinetic energy in plasma (units: eV)
**EOT**: Equivalent Oxide Thickness - physical thickness adjusted for dielectric constant (EOT = t × κ_ref / κ_material)
**ESC**: Electrostatic Chuck - contactless wafer holder using Coulombic clamping

## F

**Fluorine Affinity**: Relative ease with which material reacts with F• radicals
**Free Energy (ΔG)**: Thermodynamic measure of reaction spontaneity (negative = spontaneous)

## G

**Gain**: Device amplification factor (proportional to Cox/Cd)
**GIDL**: Gate-Induced Drain Leakage - parasitic leakage at drain corner under reverse bias
**Gm**: Transconductance - change in drain current per unit gate voltage

## H

**HfO₂**: Hafnium dioxide - most common high-κ gate dielectric in production
**High-κ**: High dielectric constant material (κ > 10, typically HfO₂ with κ ≈ 20-25)
**Hysteresis**: Shift in C-V curve due to charge trapping in interface states

## I

**IL**: Interfacial Layer - thin dielectric (typically SiO₂ or SiOₓNy) between high-κ and silicon
**ICP**: Inductively Coupled Plasma - higher density plasma source using RF coil
**Ion Energy**: Kinetic energy of ions entering sheath (determined by sheath potential)
**Ion Flux**: Number of ions arriving at surface per unit area per unit time

## L

**Leff**: Effective gate length (physical length minus etch/process losses)
**Low-κ**: Lower dielectric constant material (κ < 5, used as spacer insulators)

## M

**Macroloading**: Large-scale position-dependent etch rate variation (center vs. edge)
**Mean Free Path**: Average distance traveled by particle before collision (pressure-dependent)
**Microloading**: Feature-size-dependent etch rate variation (dense vs. isolated features)

## N

**Notching**: Lateral etch at oxide-polysilicon interface creating undercut

## P

**Plasma**: Ionized gas containing electrons, ions, and neutrals
**Polymer**: Fluorocarbon deposit (CFₓ) on surface for selectivity enhancement
**Process Window**: Range of parameters that meet all specifications

## R

**Radical**: Neutral atom/molecule with unpaired electron (e.g., F•)
**RTA**: Rapid Thermal Anneal - fast heating to 400-600°C for damage recovery
**RF**: Radio frequency power (13.56 MHz standard for oxide etch)

## S

**Selectivity**: Ratio of etch rates for two materials (e.g., SiO₂:Poly = 100:1)
**Sheath**: High-field region between plasma and electrode where ions accelerate
**SiO₂**: Silicon dioxide - thermal oxide commonly used at 28nm and legacy nodes
**SiOₓNy**: Silicon oxynitride - interfacial layer material with nitrogen for improved properties
**Sputtering**: Physical ion-induced material removal
**SS**: Subthreshold Swing - how quickly current changes with gate voltage (ideal: 60 mV/decade)

## T

**TiN**: Titanium nitride - metal gate material with moderate work function (~4.3 eV)
**Tunneling**: Quantum mechanical process where electrons pass through potential barrier

## V

**Vfb**: Flat-band voltage - gate voltage when band bending is zero
**Vth**: Threshold Voltage - gate voltage for inversion (key device parameter, typically 0.3-0.5 V)
**Volatility**: Tendency of material to evaporate (gaseous state at operating temperature)

## W

**Work Function**: Energy required to remove electron from material to vacuum

## Z

**ZrO₂**: Zirconium dioxide - alternative high-κ dielectric (slightly higher κ than HfO₂)
