# Chapter 10: Etch Rate Control & Chemistry Optimization

## 10.1 RF Power Effects (100-250W range)

**Dissociation Increase**: Higher power → More F• radicals
**Ion Generation**: Proportional to power

| Power | F• Level | Etch Rate | Ion Density |
|-------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| 100 W | Low | ~250 nm/min | Lower |
| 200 W | High | ~350 nm/min | Higher |
| 300 W | Very High | ~400+ nm/min | Very High |

**Trade-off**: Higher power improves rate but increases damage.

## 10.2 Gas Mixture Optimization

**CF₄/O₂ Ratio Tuning**:
- 80:20 ratio: Balanced approach (industry standard)
- C₄F₈ addition (5-15%): Selectivity and anisotropy enhancement

## 10.3 Pressure Tuning (50-200 mTorr)

**Low Pressure**: Better anisotropy, poorer uniformity
**High Pressure**: Better uniformity, less anisotropic

## 10.4 Temperature Dependence

**Arrhenius Relationship**: Ea ≈ 40-50 kJ/mol for oxide

**Rate Change**: ~0.3-0.8%/°C
