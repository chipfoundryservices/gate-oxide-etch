# Chapter 5: Reactor Architecture & Electrode Materials

## 5.1 Parallel-Plate CCP Design (Industry Standard)

**Configuration**:
- Upper electrode: RF-powered (13.56 MHz)
- Lower electrode: Grounded or biased
- Gap spacing: 20-50 mm
- Typical area: 300-600 mm diameter for 300mm wafers

**Advantages**:
- Simple, mature design
- Good process control
- Reproducible performance
- Industry-standard equipment

**Design Trade-offs**:
- Smaller gap → Better uniformity, higher ion energy
- Larger gap → Poorer uniformity, lower ion energy

## 5.2 ICP (Inductively Coupled Plasma) Configuration

**Coil Design**:
- Frequency: 2 MHz typical
- Turns: 6-12
- Separate from 13.56 MHz bias supply

**Advantages**:
- Decoupled ion generation (ICP coil) from ion acceleration (bias power)
- Higher plasma density
- Lower ion energy achievable
- Better for low-damage processes

## 5.3 Electrode Materials

**Aluminum (Al)**:
- Industry standard for oxide etch
- High thermal conductivity (237 W/m·K)
- Good RF properties
- Anodized surface prevents corrosion

**Material Compatibility**:
- Resists fluorine attack when anodized
- Thermal stress management critical

## 5.4 Chamber Geometry Optimization

**Critical Parameters**:
- Electrode diameter: Affects uniformity
- Gap spacing: Trade-off between uniformity and ion energy
- Wall-to-electrode distance: Affects plasma edges

## 5.5 Wafer Support Minimization

**Electrostatic Chuck (ESC)**:
- No mechanical contacts
- Eliminates particle generation
- Enables thermal control
