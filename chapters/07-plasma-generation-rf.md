# Chapter 7: Plasma Generation & RF Coupling Systems

## 7.1 13.56 MHz CCP Fundamental

**Standard plasma frequency** for most semiconductor etch.
- Efficient coupling to capacitive loads
- Well-established matching networks
- Power range: 50-500 W typical

## 7.2 Dual-Frequency Systems

**13.56 MHz + 2 MHz Combination**:
- 13.56 MHz: For ion dissociation and generation
- 2 MHz: For independent ion energy (bias) control
- Decoupled optimization of dissociation vs. acceleration

## 7.3 RF Matching Networks

**Purpose**: Impedance matching (50 Ω source to ~10-50 Ω plasma load)

**Automatic Tuning**:
- Variable capacitors (motorized)
- Feedback from reflected power sensor
- Target: <5% reflected power

## 7.4 Plasma Diagnostics

**Optical Emission Spectroscopy (OES)**:
- F• intensity (685-690 nm): Etchant indicator
- CF intensity (280-300 nm): Chemistry balance

**Langmuir Probe**:
- Electron temperature, density
- Ion energy distribution
