# Chapter 9: Vacuum Systems & Pressure Regulation

## 9.1 Turbomolecular Pump (TMP)

**Pumping Speed**: 100-1000 L/s for 300mm tools

**Blade Design**: Multi-stage compression
- High compression ratio ensures low ultimate pressure
- Typical compression: 10⁶-10⁷

## 9.2 Backing Pump Requirements

**Mechanical Pump**: 3-10 L/s backing capacity
**Dry Pump** (preferred): Oil-free, better for fluorine environment

## 9.3 Throttle Valve (Butterfly Valve)

**Function**: PID-controlled pressure regulation
- Sensor: Capacitive manometer
- Feedback: Adjusts valve opening
- Stability target: ±0.5 mTorr at 100 mTorr setpoint

## 9.4 Pressure Stability Impact

**±1% Pressure Variation** ≈ ±1-2% Etch Rate Change
**Importance**: Critical for recipe repeatability and uniformity
