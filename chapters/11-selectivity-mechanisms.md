# Chapter 11: Selectivity Engineering - Multi-Material Selectivity

## 11.1 SiO₂/Polysilicon Selectivity (>100:1 target)

**Polymer Passivation Strategy**:
- C₄F₈ co-flow deposits polymer on poly surface
- Polymer protection prevents F• attack
- Temperature control (cold = more polymer)

| Temperature | Polymer Deposition | Poly Rate | SiO₂ Rate | Selectivity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40°C | High | 2 nm/min | 280 nm/min | 140:1 |
| 60°C | Moderate | 5 nm/min | 300 nm/min | 60:1 |
| 80°C | Low | 10 nm/min | 320 nm/min | 32:1 |

## 11.2 SiO₂/TiN Selectivity Challenge

**TiN Etch Rate**: 10-30 nm/min (moderate, difficult selectivity)

**Selectivity Achievement**:
- Use Ru gate (better selectivity, faster etch)
- Low temperature (reduces TiN etch)
- High selectivity chemistries (exploratory)

## 11.3 High-κ (HfO₂) Selectivity to Underlying Oxide

**Challenge**: HfO₂ etch rate often > SiO₂ etch rate
**Solution**: Custom chemistry, possibly different plasma source
