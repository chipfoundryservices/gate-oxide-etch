# Chapter 8: Substrate Holders, Cooling, & Thermal Management

## 8.1 Electrostatic Chuck (ESC)

**Operating Principle**: Coulombic clamping force

**Specifications**:
- Voltage: 100-1000 V DC
- Clamping force: 1-5 N/mm² of wafer area
- Temperature range: -20°C to +200°C

**Advantages**:
- No particle generation (critical for advanced nodes)
- Uniform clamping
- Enables precise temperature control

## 8.2 Helium Backside Cooling

**Thermal Conductivity**:
- He at 10 Torr: ~0.1 W/m·K
- He at 40 Torr: ~0.15 W/m·K

**Temperature Uniformity**: ±2°C typical specification

## 8.3 Temperature Monitoring

**Thermocouple Integration**: Embedded in chuck backside
**IR Monitoring**: Non-contact surface measurement

## 8.4 Thermal Transients

**Heating during plasma ignition**: ~0.5-1°C/second
**Cooling after process end**: Variable depending on He flow

**Impact**: Process window tuning must account for thermal transient effects
