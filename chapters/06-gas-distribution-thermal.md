# Chapter 6: Gas Distribution, Pressure, & Temperature Control

## 6.1 Showerhead Design
- Multiple orifices (50-200) for uniform distribution
- Hole size: 0.5-2 mm
- Prevents gas channeling and dead zones

## 6.2 Mass Flow Controller Specifications
- Accuracy: ±2% of setpoint
- Response time: <1 second
- Range: 0-200 sccm typical for gate oxide etch

## 6.3 Pressure Regime Effects

### Low Pressure (50-100 mTorr)
- Long mean free path → Anisotropic etch
- Better ion directionality
- Poorer uniformity

### High Pressure (150-300 mTorr)
- Short mean free path → More isotropic
- Better uniformity (gas collisions randomize)
- Etch rate increases

## 6.4 Temperature Control Strategy

**Target Range**: 40-80°C for gate oxide etch
- Too cold: Excessive polymer, poor selectivity
- Too hot: Isotropic etch, interface damage

**Helium Backside Cooling**:
- Pressure: 10-40 Torr
- Thermal conductance control
- Enables ±2°C uniformity
