# Chapter 14: Process-Induced Damage & Interface Quality

## 14.1 Damage Mechanisms

**Ion Bombardment Damage**:
- Lattice displacement from kinetic energy
- Creates point defects and dislocations
- Typical depth: 2-10 nm subsurface

**Charge Trapping**:
- Defect states trap electrons or holes
- Shifts Vth, increases leakage
- Dit (interface state density) is key metric

## 14.2 Interface State Density (Dit) Specifications

**Low-Damage Process Target**:
- Before etch: Dit < 10¹⁰ cm⁻² eV⁻¹ (pristine SiO₂)
- After etch: Dit < 5×10¹¹ cm⁻² eV⁻¹ (controlled damage)
- After anneal: Dit < 2×10¹¹ cm⁻² eV⁻¹ (recovery)

## 14.3 Damage Mitigation Strategies

**Low-Ion-Energy Process**: 20-50 eV (vs. typical 50-100 eV)
**Lower Temperature**: 40-60°C to reduce thermal stress
**Post-Etch Annealing**: RTA at 400-500°C for Dit recovery
