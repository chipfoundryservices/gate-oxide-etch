# Chapter 16: Manufacturing Integration & Device Yield

## 16.1 Pre-Etch Process Impact

**Gate Electrode Uniformity**:
- Thickness variation <±2% for consistent etch
- Affects profile and selectivity

**Hard Mask Uniformity** (if used):
- Thickness variation <±5%

## 16.2 Post-Etch Cleaning

**Residue Removal**:
- SiF₄ byproducts: Volatile (minimal residue)
- HfF₄ byproducts: Hygroscopic (can condense)
- Wet clean: HF dip (5-10 sec) for residue removal

## 16.3 Cluster Tool Thermal Coupling

**Challenge**: Heat dissipation from etch chamber affects next chamber
**Solution**: Precondition wafer temperature before next step

## 16.4 Device Yield Correlations

**Vth Distribution**: Width correlates to etch uniformity
**Gate Leakage**: Sensitive to etch profile uniformity
**Reliability (BTI)**: Correlates to interface quality (Dit)

## 16.5 Cost and Throughput

**Typical Throughput**: 6-12 minutes per wafer (including transfer + etch)
**Etch Time**: 2-5 minutes (process-dependent)
**Chamber Maintenance**: 10-15% downtime impact
