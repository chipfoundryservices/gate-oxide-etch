# Chapter 13: Microloading, Notching, & Uniformity

## 13.1 Microloading Physics

**Radical Depletion**:
- Dense features consume more radicals locally
- Radical depletion slows etch in neighboring regions
- Result: Feature-size-dependent etch rate variation

**Typical Variation**: 10-30% etch rate difference between dense and isolated features

## 13.2 Notching Mechanism

**Definition**: Enhanced lateral etch at oxide-polysilicon interface

**Cause**: Electric field enhancement at dielectric interface

**Magnitude**: Typically 10-50 nm undercut

**Mitigation**:
- Temperature reduction (<60°C)
- Pressure increase (>100 mTorr)
- Floating shield to moderate electric field
